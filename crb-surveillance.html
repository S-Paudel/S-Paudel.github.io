Pre-border surveillance

CRB Surveillance

Every GBIF and iNaturalist record of the coconut rhinoceros beetle (Oryctes rhinoceros) in South America, the Caribbean and the continental United States, checked daily. Each record keeps the date it was first seen, so you can see exactly what is new. Many records in new regions are mis-identifications, so each one is graded by how much evidence it carries.

How it works

  1. Every day · GBIF + iNaturalist Every record of the beetle in South America, the Caribbean and the continental US is collected, plus Mexico and Central America, where the beetle is established on the Pacific coast. The continental US excludes Hawaiʻi and Alaska. GBIF copies of iNaturalist records are merged, so nothing is counted twice.
  2. Evidence grading Confirmed means research grade on iNaturalist, or confirmed by a reviewer here. Needs checking covers "needs ID" and casual records, plus museum, survey, laboratory and DNA records from GBIF. Captive / lab covers pets, cultures and interceptions.
  3. Alerts New record · first record in a country · first confirmed record · a jump of more than 100 km from any earlier confirmed record · a record upgraded to confirmed · a monthly spike (more than double the previous month) · a record withdrawn at its source. Alerts are e-mailed as GitHub issues.
  4. Human review Open any record and mark it "Confirmed CRB" or "Not CRB". Records marked "Not CRB" drop out of counts and alerts.

Sources & citation

Records come from GBIF and iNaturalist. They remain the property of their publishers and observers. Each one links back to its source. Records can be mis-identified, so check the photo before acting.
