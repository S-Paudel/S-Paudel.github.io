Giving back

Community & Volunteering

Science that stays in a journal doesn't protect anyone's crop, and policy that ignores migrant communities doesn't serve them either. Alongside my research, I take on governance, advocacy, and volunteer roles — mostly within the Multicultural community in New Zealand and regional biosecurity groups.

Governance & board roles

Adviser, Multicultural Advisory Group

Christchurch City Council · 2026–Present

Provides advice and community perspectives to Christchurch City Council on issues affecting culturally diverse communities, contributing to initiatives that promote inclusion, civic participation, social cohesion, and equitable outcomes across Christchurch.

Member

Environment Canterbury Biosecurity Advisory Group · 2026–present

Contributes local and scientific perspective to regional biosecurity planning and decision-making.

Treasurer

Non-Resident Nepali Association (NRNA) New Zealand · 2025–Present

Oversight of finances and governance compliance for a national organisation representing 40,000+ Nepalese migrants in New Zealand.

Board Member & Oceania Coordinator

International Association for the Plant Protection Sciences (IAPPS) · 2025–Present

Represents the Oceania region on the IAPPS board, connecting Pacific plant-protection science with the wider international community.

Executive member and Funding Lead

Nepal New Zealand Friendship Society of Canterbury (NNZFSC) · 2024–2026

Secured $70,000+ annually for multicultural sports, cultural, and women's skill-development programmes.

Founding President

Nepalese Student Society, Pennsylvania State University · USA · 2016–2018

Established and led the university's Nepalese student community during graduate study.

Founding Member

NRNA Youth Leadership Program · USA, 2019

Associate Editor

New Zealand Journal of Crop and Horticultural Science , 2024 - Present
