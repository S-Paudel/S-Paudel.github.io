CRB Damage Monitor

Screening public palm photos for the V-shaped frond cuts left by the coconut rhinoceros beetle (Oryctes rhinoceros). Photos stay where they are published. This page links to them and keeps only the analysis.

Everything the monitor has screened. Each photo is first seen on the date it entered the record, so you can ask for new damage since any date.

Paste one or more links, one per line. These can be an iNaturalist observation, a GBIF occurrence, a Mapillary or Flickr photo page, a direct image address, or a news page with a palm photo. Check the preview, then send them for analysis.

"Send for analysis" opens a pre-filled GitHub issue. Submit it and the analysis runs automatically. The issue gets a reply with the result, and the result appears under Results. Links from people outside the project wait until a maintainer approves them.

How a photo is screened

  1. 01 · Search filters Each source is asked only for photos in the chosen areas and dates. Photos must have open licences. iNaturalist and GBIF are limited to records identified as coconut palm. Photos already screened are skipped, so each scan only looks at what is new.
  2. 02 · Thinning Street-level sequences repeat the same palms every few metres. Mapillary keeps one image per ~150 m per day.
  3. 03 · Palm check (cheap) A small image–text model (CLIP) looks at the thumbnail and asks "is a palm crown visible?". Beetles, grubs, coconuts, people and palm-free streets are dropped here. This takes a fraction of a second per photo on an ordinary CPU.
  4. 04 · Damage detector (expensive) Only photos that pass go to Aubrey Moore's detector. SAM3 outlines each coconut palm. The outline is smoothed with elliptic Fourier descriptors, and the gaps between the smooth and real outlines are candidate V-cuts. A shape classifier trained by Aubrey then keeps the cut-shaped ones. Palms touching the photo edge are flagged and their cuts are not counted.
  5. 05 · Human review Results are screening only. Open any photo and confirm or reject it. Your decision is saved with the record.
