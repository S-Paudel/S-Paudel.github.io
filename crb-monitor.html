Biosecurity tool

CRB Damage Monitor

Screening public coconut palm photos from South America, the Caribbean and the continental United States for the V-shaped frond cuts left by the coconut rhinoceros beetle (Oryctes rhinoceros). Photos stay where they are published. This page links to them and keeps only the analysis.

→ For new beetle records from GBIF and iNaturalist, see the CRB Surveillance dashboard

Coconut palm photos from iNaturalist in the watch regions, screened for V-shaped frond cuts with Aubrey Moore's detector. Each photo is first seen on the date it entered the record, so you can ask for new damage since any date.

Paste one or more links, one per line. These can be an iNaturalist observation, a GBIF occurrence, a direct image address, or a news page with a palm photo. Check the preview, then send them for analysis.

"Send for analysis" opens a pre-filled issue in the project's private GitHub repository. Submit it and the analysis runs automatically. The issue gets a reply with the result, and the result appears under Palm damage results. Sending needs access to that repository, so if you would like to contribute, contact Sulav Paudel.

How a palm photo is screened

  1. 01 · Search filters iNaturalist is asked only for openly licensed photos identified as coconut palm, in the watch regions and dates, and added since the last scan. Photos already screened are skipped.
  2. 02 · Palm check (cheap) A small image–text model (CLIP) looks at the thumbnail and asks "is a palm crown visible?". Nuts, flowers, trunks, people and palm-free scenes are dropped. This takes a fraction of a second per photo on an ordinary CPU.
  3. 03 · Damage detector (expensive) Only photos that pass go to Aubrey Moore's detector. SAM3 outlines each coconut palm. The outline is smoothed with elliptic Fourier descriptors, and the gaps between the smooth and real outlines are candidate V-cuts. A shape classifier trained by Aubrey keeps the cut-shaped ones. Palms touching the photo edge are flagged and their cuts are not counted.
  4. 04 · Human review Results are screening only. Open any photo and confirm or reject it.
