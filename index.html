01

Personal

I grew up in Nepal, where agriculture is not an abstraction — it's the work that feeds a household. That's the lens I've carried through every posting since: from coordinating smallholder programs in Kathmandu, to a PhD in entomology in Pennsylvania, to tracking an invasive beetle across the South Pacific from a lab in Lincoln, New Zealand.

I'm drawn to problems that sit between science and people — where good data only matters if it reaches a farmer, a customs officer, or a community group in time to act on it. Outside of formal research, that same instinct shows up as public engagement, mentoring, and volunteering with regional biosecurity groups (see Community).

As a scientist from the Global South working in international development, I've also written about the practical barriers — visas, travel, and access — that shape whose science gets to travel and whose doesn't. It's part of why I care about building capacity locally, not just publishing from afar.

02

Professional

Senior Scientist in applied entomology at the Bioeconomy Science Institute (formerly AgResearch), specialising in transboundary and invasive pest surveillance, biological control, and integrated pest management (IPM) across the Pacific region.

Career timeline

  1. Senior Scientist — Entomology & International Development

    Bioeconomy Science Institute (formerly AgResearch) · Lincoln, NZ

    Leads applied entomology and biosecurity surveillance work across the South Pacific, with a focus on the coconut rhinoceros beetle (Oryctes rhinoceros) and other transboundary pests.

  2. Post-Doctoral Scientist

    AgResearch Ltd. · New Zealand

    Research on biological control agents and IPM strategies for invasive insect pests in Pacific Island agricultural systems.

  3. Ph.D. & M.Sc., Entomology and International Agriculture & Development

    Pennsylvania State University · USA

    Dual-title graduate research spanning insect-plant interactions, climate change, and international agricultural development, alongside fieldwork across roughly twenty countries.

  4. IPM Program Manager

    International Development Enterprises (iDE) · Kathmandu, Nepal

    Co-principal investigator and site coordinator for the USAID-funded Integrated Pest Management and Horticulture Innovation Labs; coordinated the five-year USAID PAHAL project supporting smallholder farmers.

  5. B.Sc. (Hons) Agriculture

    Tribhuwan University · Rampur, Nepal
