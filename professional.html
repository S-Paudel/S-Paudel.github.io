Career

Professional

Applied entomologist, biosecurity scientist, and international development practitioner with 13+ years of experience in pest management, biological control, invasive species surveillance, and agricultural innovation. My work combines field research, data science, spatial analysis, and statistical modelling to support evidence-based decisions for biosecurity, food systems, and sustainable agriculture. I currently lead coconut rhinoceros beetle surveillance and biocontrol programmes across the South Pacific and contribute to pest surveillance initiatives for New Zealand's pastoral systems.

Education

  1. Ph.D., Entomology

    The Pennsylvania State University · University Park, Pennsylvania, USA

    Dissertation focused on insect-plant interactions, climate change, and crop losses from insect pests, with applications to food security and agricultural sustainability.

  2. M.Sc., Entomology & International Agriculture and Development

    The Pennsylvania State University · University Park, Pennsylvania, USA

    Dual-title master's programme combining applied entomology with international agricultural development, focusing on sustainable agriculture and global food systems.

  3. B.Sc. (Hons.), Agriculture

    Tribhuvan University · Rampur, Nepal

    Undergraduate training in agricultural sciences, providing a foundation in crop production, pest management, agricultural economics, and rural development.

Career timeline

  1. Senior Scientist — Entomology & International Development

    Bioeconomy Science Institute (formerly AgResearch) · Lincoln, NZ

    Leads applied research in biosecurity, pest management, and agricultural development, combining field science, data analytics, and stakeholder engagement to address complex biological and environmental challenges.

  2. Post-Doctoral Scientist

    AgResearch Ltd. · New Zealand

    Research on biological control agents and IPM strategies for invasive insect pests in Pacific Island agricultural systems.

  3. Agriculture Program Manager

    International Development Enterprises (iDE) · Kathmandu, Nepal

    Led the agricultural component of USAID's $13M PAHAL project, improving livelihoods for 100,000 rural households. Designed and implemented the $2M ENBAITA programme, expanding agricultural input access for 50,000 farmers, and managed USAID's IPM Innovation Lab and Horticulture Innovation Lab programmes, reaching 62,000 households with eco-friendly pest management technologies. Co-principal investigator and site coordinator for both Innovation Labs.

    Moscow State Agro-Engineering University · Russia | Apr 2012 – May 2012
